# Category & advertiser insights — where should sales focus?

*Simulated data. Not Swiggy data. Window: weeks 19–22 (31 Aug – 27 Sep 2026), compared with weeks 15–18. This window was picked to sit before the festive spike; the bot week's extra clicks only affect the Whitefield zone line. Sources: `marts.rpt_category_weekly`, `marts.rpt_zone_weekly`, `marts.fct_advertiser_weekly`, `marts.rpt_inventory_weekly`, `marts.rpt_cohort_retention`.*

**Bottom line:** Revenue is supply-limited by **under-delivering budgets, not by demand**. 349 advertisers with ROAS above 4x spend under 40% of their budgets, leaving about ₹39.6 L/week unspent. Fixing their bids and targeting is the biggest lever. On the risk side, **₹17.0 L (17%) of four-week revenue comes from 375 advertisers with ROAS below 2x**. Those accounts will churn unless we fix their performance.

## 2. Advertisers — three call lists

**a) Budget-capped winners: ask for more budget.** 33 advertisers spend over 95% of budget at over 5x ROAS (₹8.4 L of four-week spend). They are leaving demand on the table.

| The Beer Cafe | chain, Chinese | 8.7x | 100% |
| Curry Leaves | chain, Andhra | 5.1x | 99% |

**b) Under-delivering high-ROAS accounts: fix bids and targeting.** 349 advertisers have ROAS above 4x but spend under 40% of their budget, about ₹39.6 L/week unspent against ₹2.9 L/week spent. Examples: Happy Milk (dairy, 9.4x ROAS, 1% used), The Black Pearl (17.4x, 3%), Iceland (frozen, 4.5x, 2%), American Garden (5.1x, 16%). Their bids lose auctions or their targeting is too narrow. **Unlocking even 10% of this is about ₹4 L/week, roughly +16% revenue.**

**c) Churn-risk spenders: fix performance first.** 375 advertisers below 2x ROAS hold 17% of revenue. The largest are Valley Spice (staples, 0.7x, ₹2.2 L in four weeks), Kohinoor (1.2x), Farmland (frozen, 0.5x), Kingfisher (beverages, 1.0x) and Chai Darbaar (1.3x). The simulator's churn model, like real advertisers, cuts budget after weeks of poor ROAS.

## 3. Zones

Revenue follows population: Koramangala ₹9.1 L, Whitefield ₹8.8 L and Indiranagar ₹8.1 L lead, with Yelahanka lowest at ₹4.1 L. ROAS is a narrow 4.1–5.1x everywhere, so **no zone is broken**. Jayanagar (5.1x) and Bellandur (5.0x) earn the most per rupee and are the easiest upsell pitch. Whitefield's +6.6% growth includes the week-21 bot (WBR 21), and its underlying growth is about flat. Fill is lowest in Rajajinagar and Banashankari (89–90%), the zones to target for new restaurant sign-ups.

## 4. Retention

Of advertisers who start spending in a given week, 68% are active a week later, 65% after 4 weeks and 60% after 8 (cohorts from weeks 2–18). Most drop-off happens in the first two weeks, so onboarding (first-campaign setup, bid guidance) matters more than late-stage retention.
