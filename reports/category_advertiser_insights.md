# Category & advertiser insights — where should sales focus?

*Simulated data. Not Swiggy data. Window: weeks 19–22 (31 Aug – 27 Sep 2026), compared with weeks 15–18. This window was picked to sit before the festive spike; the bot week's extra clicks only affect the Whitefield zone line. Sources: `marts.rpt_category_weekly`, `marts.rpt_zone_weekly`, `marts.fct_advertiser_weekly`, `marts.rpt_inventory_weekly`, `marts.rpt_cohort_retention`.*

**Bottom line:** Revenue is supply-limited by **under-delivering budgets, not by demand**. 361 advertisers with ROAS above 4x spend under 40% of their budgets, leaving about ₹43.4 L/week unspent. Fixing their bids and targeting is the biggest lever. On the risk side, **₹20.1 L (20%) of four-week revenue comes from 388 advertisers with ROAS below 2x**. Those accounts will churn unless we fix their performance.

## 2. Advertisers — three call lists

**a) Budget-capped winners: ask for more budget.** 36 advertisers spend over 95% of budget at over 5x ROAS (₹8.2 L of four-week spend). They are leaving demand on the table.

**b) Under-delivering high-ROAS accounts: fix bids and targeting.** 361 advertisers have ROAS above 4x but spend under 40% of their budget, about ₹43.4 L/week unspent against ₹3.3 L/week spent. Examples: Happy Milk (dairy, 10.6x ROAS, 0.5% used), The Biere Club (13.9x, 14%), Loreal Paris (9.7x, 4%), Cadbury Oreo (7.1x, 1%). Their bids lose auctions or their targeting is too narrow. **Unlocking even 10% of this is about ₹4.3 L/week, roughly +17% revenue.**

**c) Churn-risk spenders: fix performance first.** 388 advertisers below 2x ROAS hold 20% of revenue. The largest are Ratan (household, 0.7x, ₹1.4 L in four weeks), Tata Sampann (staples, 0.6x), Sugar free (0.9x), Hotel Karavali (1.6x) and Farmland (frozen, 0.5x). The simulator's churn model, like real advertisers, cuts budget after weeks of poor ROAS.

## 3. Zones

Revenue follows population: Koramangala ₹9.2 L, Whitefield ₹8.9 L and Indiranagar ₹8.1 L lead, with Yelahanka lowest at ₹4.1 L. ROAS is a narrow 4.2–5.0x everywhere, so **no zone is broken**. Koramangala and Bellandur (both 5.0x) earn the most per rupee and are the easiest upsell pitch. Whitefield's +2.7% growth is entirely the week-21 bot (WBR 21). Fill is lowest in Rajajinagar, Banashankari, Bellandur and Marathahalli (92.0–92.5%), the zones to target for new restaurant sign-ups.

## 4. Retention

Of advertisers who start spending in a given week, 68% are active a week later, 63% after 4 weeks and 62% after 8 (cohorts from weeks 2–18). Most drop-off happens in the first week, so onboarding (first-campaign setup, bid guidance) matters more than late-stage retention.
